1. Overview

At SAEM Security, accessible from www.saemsecurity.com, safeguarding your privacy is a core priority. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that is collected and recorded by SAEM Security and how we use it.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide to us when you fill out our contact form, communicate with us via email, or request our cybersecurity services. This information may include:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Subject and content of your message
  • Any additional details regarding your business security needs that you choose to provide

3. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect in various ways, including to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our website and services
  • Respond to your comments, questions, and requests via our support team
  • Communicate with you regarding our penetration testing, vulnerability analysis, and reporting services
  • Prevent fraudulent activity and enhance system security

4. Employee Information Privacy

We strictly protect the privacy and personal details of our team members, analysts, and staff. SAEM Security does not share, disclose, or distribute employee personal information, internal credentials, or private staff records with external third parties, unauthorized entities, or the general public under any circumstances.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website utilizes local storage strictly to save your preferred site theme (dark or light mode). We do not use tracking cookies or persistent advertising cookies to track your browsing habits across external platforms.

6. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party providers to support our operations:

  • Formspree: Manages our secure contact form submissions. Data submitted through forms is processed according to Formspree's privacy practices.
  • Google reCAPTCHA: Protects our website from spam and automated abuse. This service is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • Google Fonts: Serves typography styles securely to our interface.

7. Data Security

As a corporate cybersecurity company, we implement rigorous technical, administrative, and physical security measures designed to protect your personal data and business communication from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request correction of any inaccurate information, or ask for your data to be deleted. To exercise these rights, please contact us directly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We encourage you to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately upon being posted to this page.
